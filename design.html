The Bar

Three glasses that anchor the room.

Nº 01 · The Ritual
Coffee
Ceremony
bunna · ቡና

Green beans roasted table-side on a clay brazier, ground by hand, brewed in a jebena. Three rounds — abol, tona, baraka — poured with popcorn and himbasha. Frankincense in the air.

£12 · for the table
Nº 02 · The House
Tej
honey wine · ጠጅ

Fermented slowly with gesho leaves — a wild hop-like herb that gives it a light bitterness on the finish. Golden, sweet-dry, poured into a berele. The house pour, and the only one you'll find on most London bars.

£8 · glass  ·  £30 · carafe
Nº 03 · The Modern
Berbere
Paloma
house cocktail

Blanco tequila, pink grapefruit, lime, a soda top — built over a rim of berbere salt that catches the corner of your mouth on the first sip. It shouldn't work. It does. On the menu since we opened.

£11 · one for the road
